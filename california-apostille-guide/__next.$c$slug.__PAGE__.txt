16:T17f7,<p>Direct Answer</p><p>A <strong>California apostille</strong> is a single-step authentication issued by the California Secretary of State under the 1961 Hague Convention. It applies to eligible California vital records, notarized documents, academic records, and California-issued public documents. Filed at the Sacramento or Los Angeles counter, or by mail.</p><p><a href="/apostille-document-check/">Verify Your Document — $45 Check</a></p><h2>What Documents Qualify</h2><p>Apostille applies to public documents issued in California. The four eligibility buckets:</p><ul><li><strong>Vital records</strong> — birth, marriage, death certificates from CDPH or county recorder (long-form copies for most foreign uses). See <a href="/which-documents-can-be-apostilled/">complete eligibility taxonomy</a>.</li><li><strong>Court records</strong> — divorce decrees, court orders. Often need county-clerk certification of the judge’s signature before the apostille.</li><li><strong>Notarized documents</strong> — POAs, affidavits, statements. The Secretary of State first certifies the notary’s commission, then attaches the apostille.</li><li><strong>Academic + business records</strong> — diplomas, transcripts, articles of incorporation, certificates of good standing.</li></ul><h2>Federal Documents — Not California</h2><p>FBI background checks, USCIS records, IRS letters, and other federal documents are authenticated by the <strong>U.S. Department of State Office of Authentications</strong> in Washington, D.C. — not by California. We coordinate federal routing separately. <a href="/fbi-background-check-apostille-california/">Federal routing →</a></p><h2>The Filing Process</h2><ol><li>Confirm eligibility via the <a href="/apostille-document-check/">$45 Document Check</a>.</li><li>Choose filing route — <a href="/same-day-apostille-california/">same-day Sacramento walk-in</a> or <a href="/california-apostille-by-mail/">mail submission</a>.</li><li>Secretary of State attaches the apostille (counter: typically same business day when capacity allows; mail: see <a href="https://www.sos.ca.gov/notary/processing-times" rel="noopener">live posted times</a>).</li><li>Document returns with apostille attached, ready for the destination country.</li><li>Translation, if required by the destination, is completed <em>after</em> the apostille — see the relevant <a href="/apostille-by-country/">country page</a>.</li></ol><h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2><p>Two California Secretary of State locations: the Sacramento public counter at 1500 11th Street, 3rd Floor, and the Los Angeles regional office at 300 South Spring Street, Room 12513.
