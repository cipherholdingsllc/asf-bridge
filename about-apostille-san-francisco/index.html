Check My Document

An apostille is a government-issued authentication certificate that makes your California documents legally valid in foreign countries. It is not the same as notarization — apostille is a separate process handled by the California Secretary of State, not a notary. We manage the full submission so you don’t have to navigate the SOS process yourself.
